## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration** | A site superintendent can log progress, issues and sign-offs from their phone, on the spot, faster than sending a group text. Nobody in the office has to re-key it. Winning means foremen pick Meridian over WhatsApp because it makes their day easier, not because head office makes them use it. |
| **Where to play** | Who: superintendents and foremen on $50M+ projects within existing enterprise accounts (the 38 top-100 US general contractors already using Meridian). Where: US only. Surface: mobile-first and offline-capable, added to the core platform rather than replacing it. Use cases: daily site logs, issues and snags documented with photos, and capturing RFIs and change orders. Not in scope (for now): mid-market ($5M–$50M) acquisition, subcontractor-only accounts, or desktop. Mid-market comes later, once the field experience is proven and can serve as the wedge to win those firms back. |
| **How to win** | Meridian is already the system of record for 38 of the top 100 US contractors. What you capture on site goes straight into that record: each photo or voice note is tagged automatically to the project, location, drawing and schedule activity. One tap on site becomes structured data that feeds the compliance, RFI and budget-variance workflows those firms already run on. Point tools like camera apps, group chats and mobile-first competitors can capture but can’t connect to the system of record. Enterprise competitors have the depth but not the field UX. Meridian is the only one that can offer both. |
| **Capabilities required** | World-class at: mobile UX for harsh conditions, meaning offline-first, camera- and voice-first, and usable with gloves and in bright sun. Build: one capture layer (photo, voice and quick status) that writes into existing Meridian objects. Buy or partner: speech-to-text, plus drawing and BIM integrations. Team: UX research on live sites every sprint, and customer success running field pilots with 3–5 enterprise accounts. |
| **Management systems** | North star: weekly field-active users per project, measured as the % of site leads logging 3 or more days a week. Supporting metrics: time from capture to office record, and the % of issues that start in the app rather than being re-keyed. Guardrails: enterprise renewal rates and office admin NPS hold steady, and office admin usage of enterprise features doesn’t drop. Rituals: UX research on live sites every sprint, a monthly field adoption review across pilot projects, and a quarterly review with pilot accounts linking field adoption to renewal and expansion. |

## 2. Your one hard no

> **Hard no:** We will not expand enterprise analytics, ERP integrations or a top-10 account push during Foundations. Existing features stay fully supported, as contracts require, but new investment goes to the field. Deeper analytics on data that field teams never enter just builds on a gap. Field capture makes every enterprise feature more valuable, because the data finally reflects what’s happening on site.

## 3. OKR cascade

> **Objective:** Make Meridian the way field teams record site work on our $50M+ enterprise accounts, replacing phone cameras and group texts.
>
> - **KR1:** Share of site leads (superintendents and foremen) logging in Meridian 3+ days a week on pilot projects: 12% to 50% within two quarters.
> - **KR2:** Share of site issues and snags raised in the field app rather than re-keyed by the office: 20% to 70% within two quarters.
> - **KR3:** Median time from site capture to office record: 2 days to under 2 hours within two quarters.
